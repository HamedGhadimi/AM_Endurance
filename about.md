## Hamed Ghadimi, Ph.D.

**Additive manufacturing materials & process engineer**

I'm a materials and process engineer working on metal powder bed fusion, both laser (L-PBF) and electron beam (EB-PBF). What interests me most is how it all connects: parameters to melt pool, melt pool to microstructure and defects, and all of that to whether a part can be qualified.

## My path

I began as a structural engineer. In 2019 I went back to school for a Ph.D. in materials science and engineering at Louisiana State University, focused on metal parts made by additive manufacturing. My research looked at the mechanical and microstructural properties of metal AM parts, and how process parameters and post-processing shape them. Along the way I also earned a master's in computer science, applying machine learning and statistical analysis to AM data.

After graduating, I worked on AM for medical implants at ARCH Medical Solutions, qualifying L-PBF and EB-PBF processes. I then joined Seurat Technologies, where I helped develop industrial-scale L-PBF processes for Seurat's area printing technology. Today I'm a Staff Additive Manufacturing Engineer at Johnson & Johnson MedTech (DePuy Synthes), still working on process development, validation and qualification, now for medical implants, one of the most demanding applications of metal AM.

## What I work on

- L-PBF and EB-PBF process development and qualification
- Defect root-cause analysis
- Powder management and traceability
- Microstructure, mechanical and fatigue characterization
- Statistical data analysis and machine learning for AM

## Why AM Endurance

In metal AM, everything affects everything. I started these maps to make sense of that for myself, and to have a reference I could keep coming back to. Sharing them felt like the natural next step.

They're a work in progress, and I'm sure they have mistakes. If you find one, I'd be grateful to hear about it.
