## Why "AM Endurance"

AM is additive manufacturing. Endurance comes from fatigue endurance: a part's ability to keep performing, cycle after cycle. The idea behind the name is simple: in AM, knowledge is power. The better you understand the process and the material, the more you get out of the technology.

## What it is

Interactive knowledge maps of metal powder bed fusion (L-PBF and EB-PBF), written for practicing engineers and researchers. Each map covers one area, such as powder, the melt pool, defects or inspection, and shows how its concepts, mechanisms, parameters and standards connect. [Browse the maps →]({{ '/maps/' | relative_url }})

## How the maps are made

Every map starts with a clear scope. Each element is then written and tied to peer-reviewed literature or ASTM/ISO standards. Numbers come with the conditions they were measured under, and where the science is still debated, the map says so.

I use AI tools to help draft and organize content, but I check every claim against its source myself, and any errors are mine. The maps draw only on published sources, never on proprietary or employer information.

## Reading a map

Click any element to see its description and source. The legend explains the colors and shapes. Use zoom-fit to see the whole map, or open it fullscreen.

## Mistakes happen

These maps aren't perfect, and they'll never be truly finished. If something looks wrong or missing, please tell me, ideally with a source. I appreciate every correction and will update the map.
